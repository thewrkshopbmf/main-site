A seven-day practice rhythm

One focus a day

  1. Day 1: Notice the trigger.
  2. Day 2: Separate fact from story.
  3. Day 3: Name what feels threatened.
  4. Day 4: Pause before responding.
  5. Day 5: Rehearse a healthier response.
  6. Day 6: Practice repair where appropriate.
  7. Day 7: Review what you learned.

Optional biblical lens: Proverbs 4:23; James 1:19–20; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Romans 12:2. Scripture invites thoughtful discernment, not simplistic labels or blame.
