The Reflex vs. the Response: what comes out of you first?

A ready-to-run men’s Bible study on what pressure reveals and how Jesus forms new reflexes. Free for any men’s group or church.

ShareTextEmail

The teaching

It happened fast. Someone cut you off, disrespected you, ignored you or pressed you, and before you could think, something came out: frustration, defensiveness, sarcasm, anger, withdrawal, fear or control. Not planned. Automatic. Afterward you wonder, “Why did I react like that?”

Most men think character is revealed in calm moments. It isn’t. Character is revealed in interruption, because interruption bypasses performance. Pressure doesn’t create what’s inside you. It reveals it (Luke 6:45). And whatever fills your heart eventually becomes your reflex.

Your first reaction reveals what your soul has practiced most.
