Privacy & safeguarding

What stays with you, and where to get help.

A plain-language account of what the Prayer Formation pages do and don't collect.

What you type and choose stays on your page

The Prayer Pattern Finder, Surrender Tool, guided prayer and seven days run entirely in your browser. What you select or type is never sent to TheWrkShop or anyone else, and it isn't saved on your device. Close or refresh the page and it's gone. There are no accounts and no prayer-request forms in this section.

If you use a share, copy or print button, your device sends or prints only what you chose, to whoever you chose.

What the website itself collects

  • Visit counts. Like the rest of thewrkshop.com, these pages use Google Analytics to count visits. Analytics may set cookies in your browser. On these pages, the address sent to Analytics leaves off everything after a “?” or “#”, so your tool choices aren't included.
  • Share counts. When you use a share button on an article, Analytics records that something was shared and how (for example, “copied”). It doesn't record the text.
  • Fonts. Some site fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested).

Questions? Contact us.

This isn't emergency help

These resources are for spiritual reflection. They don't diagnose anything, they aren't counseling or therapy, and they don't speak for God or explain why a prayer hasn't been answered. Nobody monitors this site for requests or emergencies.

  • In danger right now? Call 911.
  • Thinking about suicide, or worried about someone who is? In the U.S., call or text 988 any time.
  • Abuse or harm? Get to safety and contact local authorities. Prayer should never be used to keep someone in danger, to silence abuse, or to pressure reconciliation.
  • Ongoing struggles? Talk with a pastor you trust and, where it fits, a doctor or licensed counselor. Getting help is part of faithfulness, not a lack of faith.

For group leaders

The group guide includes ground rules, confidentiality limits and a step-by-step response if someone discloses harm. Follow your church's safeguarding process and any reporting duties that apply to you. This website has no reporting service.
