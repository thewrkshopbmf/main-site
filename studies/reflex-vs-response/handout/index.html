Interruption→Reflex→Response

Reflex: automatic, fast, conditioned, emotional. Response: intentional, Spirit-led, formed, submitted.

You don’t rise to your intentions under pressure. You fall to your training.

Reflex Test

When I’m interrupted, pressured or frustrated, I usually:

☐ React automatically   ☐ Defend myself immediately   ☐ Escalate emotionally   ☐ Pause and respond intentionally

Trigger → Truth → Response: pressure or interruption → the fruit of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22–23) → “I don’t let pressure lead me. I respond from formation.”

The 3-Second Reflex Interrupt

  1. Pause. Not forever. Just three seconds. Most damage happens fast.
  2. Notice. What rose first? Anger, fear, control, defensiveness, withdrawal?
  3. Name it. Ask, “What is this revealing in me?” Not, “What did they do?”
  4. Replace it. Flesh says, “I need control.” The Spirit says, “Trust God.” Flesh says, “Defend yourself.” The Spirit says, “Respond with wisdom.” Flesh says, “Escalate.” The Spirit says, “Slow down.”
  5. Repeat. New reflexes are built through repetition under pressure.
