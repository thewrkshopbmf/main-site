The Pleaser · Approval and boundaries

Why is it so hard to say no?

Keeping everyone happy can quietly turn into neglecting honest commitments and limits.

Start the 7-day practice Not sure this is you? Take the Pattern Finder
What it looks like

Seeking acceptance by minimizing your own needs

What may feel threatened

  • Someone being disappointed
  • Being excluded
  • A difficult disagreement
  • Being seen as selfish

Common first reactions

  • Say yes too quickly
  • Hide my real opinion
  • Apologize unnecessarily
  • Overextend myself

What often follows

  • Feel resentful
  • Feel relieved briefly
  • Lose time or energy
  • Avoid future honesty
Where it shows up

Where this might show up

At workYou agree to extra work while already overwhelmed.
In relationshipsYou hide a disagreement to avoid tension with your spouse.
At homeYou give your child permission because you fear being disliked.

Examples are illustrative, not proof of anyone’s motives. Responding firmly to real mistreatment can be appropriate.

Look beneath the reaction

Whose disappointment am I trying to prevent?

Pause and ask: Would I still choose this if I were not afraid of disapproval?

Practice a different response

Offer a respectful answer that is true, not merely agreeable.

Use: “I need to think about that before I commit.”
