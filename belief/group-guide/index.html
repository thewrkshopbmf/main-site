The Belief Project / Group Guide
For churches, ministries and small groups

Six-session belief series

Use this resource in men’s groups, women’s groups, mixed groups, or one-on-one discipleship. Assessment responses stay private unless participants freely choose to share them.

1Notice2Examine3Respond4PracticeGroup guide

Suggested 50-minute format

  1. Connect Opening question and welcome 5 min
  2. Word Read Scripture in context and teach 12 min
  3. Search Small groups of 3–5, with permission to pass 20 min
  4. Response Private reflection and guided prayer 8 min
  5. Send One voluntary next step and blessing 5 min

For a 70-minute gathering, extend the textual study and scenario discussion. Do not ask leaders to interpret participants’ private assessment results.

The six sessions

Session 1 I Believe; Help My Unbelief

Primary texts: Mark 9:14–29

Word · textual study: What does the father confess, and what does Jesus actually do?

Search · group question: When have you confused an uncertain outcome with a reason to withdraw from God?

Response · weekly practice: Pray honestly about one area of weak trust.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Session 2 When Experience Challenges Belief

Primary texts: Psalm 73; Proverbs 3:5–6

Word · textual study: How does Asaph’s perspective change across the psalm?

Search · group question: Where might an experience be shaping your interpretation of God?

Response · weekly practice: Write a lament that distinguishes facts from conclusions.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Session 3 What Am I Really Trusting?

Primary texts: Matthew 6:19–34

Word · textual study: How do treasure, masters, and anxiety relate in Jesus’ teaching?

Search · group question: What desire becomes especially powerful under pressure?

Response · weekly practice: Name one responsibility and one outcome to release.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Session 4 When Belief Becomes Costly

Primary texts: James 1:22–25; Luke 6:46

Word · textual study: What distinction do these passages draw between hearing and doing?

Search · group question: Where is an act of obedience difficult right now?

Response · weekly practice: Choose one small, wise action.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Session 5 Bring Your Unbelief to Jesus

Primary texts: Mark 9:24; Hebrews 4:14–16

Word · textual study: How does approaching Jesus differ from manufacturing certainty?

Search · group question: What have you been hesitant to pray honestly?

Response · weekly practice: Write a personal Mark 9:24 prayer.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Session 6 Faith That Keeps Growing

Primary texts: John 15:1–11; Hebrews 12:1–3

Word · textual study: What do abiding and endurance require?

Search · group question: Which practice will help you keep returning to Christ?

Response · weekly practice: Build a realistic ongoing rhythm.

Facilitator note: Read the full passage in context, invite voluntary sharing, and do not treat responses as proof of hidden motives or deficient faith.

Before the first session

  • Share the Belief Under Pressure assessment as an optional, private starting point.
  • Set expectations: confidentiality, permission to pass, and no fixing each other.
  • Know where to point someone who is grieving deeply or in crisis: a pastor, a counselor, or emergency help.
