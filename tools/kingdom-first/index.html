The Kingdom First Diagnostic

What’s really first?

“But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well.”

Matthew 6:33

A private reflection on what may be governing your decisions, reactions, desires and trust, and what seeking His kingdom could look like in your next decision.

For leaders

Use this with a group

Invite participants to complete the reflection privately before you meet. Don’t require anyone to reveal answers, results or personal experiences. Discuss Matthew 6:19–34 together and invite voluntary sharing about one faithful next step.

A 45-minute gathering

  • 5 minWelcome and private reflection
  • 10 minScripture teaching from Matthew 6:19–34
  • 20 minSmall-group discussion
  • 5 minPersonal commitment: one faithful next step
  • 5 minPrayer

Discussion prompts

  1. What changes when a good desire becomes a demand?
  2. How can we tell the difference between responsibility and control?
  3. How does the Father’s care in Matthew 6:32 shape our response to uncertainty?

Private by design: what you enter stays in this browser tab and is never sent to TheWrkShop or any server. This is a Christian spiritual reflection tool, not a validated psychological assessment, a diagnosis, pastoral judgment, or a measure of salvation. For group use, share only what participants freely choose to share. If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, please reach out to a pastor, a counselor, or emergency services.
