What do you believe when belief becomes costly?

We can sincerely affirm God’s faithfulness and still struggle to trust Him when the outcome is uncertain. These tools help you tell apart the circumstance, the meaning you attach to it, the desire under pressure, and the next faithful response.

Important. These tools are for voluntary biblical self-reflection, not clinical assessment, pastoral judgment, or determining salvation. Grief, fear, uncertainty, and unanswered prayer are not automatically evidence of sin. You can skip questions, avoid entering names, and keep results private: your answers stay in your browser.

For churches and small groups

Using it with a group

  1. Share the assessment link ahead of time. Participation is optional.
  2. Ask everyone to reflect privately on one theme that stood out. Never require anyone to share results.
  3. Lead the session from the facilitator guide.
  4. Discuss in groups of three to five, with permission to pass.
  5. Close with one voluntary next step and prayer.
