Who We Are

TheWrkShop isn’t about one person. It’s about the work: helping men be formed into the likeness of Christ, and sending them out to be a blessing to the people around them.

Our Mission

Most men don’t fall apart in public. They drift in private. TheWrkShop helps men face the wound beneath the wound: the battle that starts inside a man long before it shows up at work, at home or in the rooms he leads.

Our Vision

Men who are the same in private as they are in public, and who make every room they enter a place where truth is easier to tell.

Our Values

  • Truth over image. We’d rather be honest than impressive.
  • Daily faithfulness. Formation happens in small daily reps, not big moments.
  • Brotherhood, not an audience. Men grow in rooms where they’re known.
  • Accountability without humiliation. We hold the line, and we hold each other up.
  • Christ is Lord of the room. Not success, not approval, not comfort.
