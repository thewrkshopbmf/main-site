Who gets access to your weight?

“Carry each other’s burdens.”

Galatians 6:2

Not everything is shared. Not everyone is safe. A private, interactive tool for men who carry it alone: who has earned your story, and at what level?

Share Text Email
The teaching

Privacy is not sin. Isolation is.

When Paul says God’s power is made perfect in weakness, he’s saying, “I stopped proving. I started receiving.” Many men hear something else: “Lower your guard in a world that hasn’t earned your trust.” Their pushback isn’t only pride. It’s pattern recognition. They’ve seen openness turn into gossip, struggle become a measuring stick and confession become a character indictment.

But the Bible never teaches “tell everyone everything.” Jesus spoke in parables publicly and explained privately. He shared His burden selectively. In Gethsemane He didn’t take all twelve. He took Peter, James and John. That isn’t fear. It’s wisdom (Matthew 7:6).

So the real question isn’t, “Should men share?” It’s, “With whom, and at what level?” Men don’t need more openness. They need safer structures. Paul didn’t stop proving to everyone. He stopped proving to God. Once that settled, he could discern who was safe, where to be honest and when to be silent. That isn’t isolation. That’s mature manhood.

Root check. Surface: “I don’t want to look weak.” Deeper: “The last time I was honest, it cost me.” The truth that breaks it: the tragedy isn’t that men wear armor. It’s when no one has proven worthy of helping them take it off.

For leaders

Use this with a men’s group

This is a good first conversation for a new group, because it gives men permission to go slow. Never press anyone past the level he chooses. Teach from 2 Corinthians 12:7–10 and Matthew 26:36–38.

A 45-minute gathering

  • 5 minWelcome. Read aloud: “Privacy is not sin. Isolation is.”
  • 10 minTeaching: the man under the load, and who Jesus took into Gethsemane
  • 10 minThe four levels of sharing. Go around the room at Level 1 only: “What kind of season are you in?”
  • 15 minGroups of three: “What makes it hard for you to share? What would make a man safe to you?”
  • 5 minPray Galatians 6:2. Agree as a group on confidentiality.

Discussion prompts

  1. When has being open backfired on you? What did you learn from it, good or bad?
  2. Why do you think Jesus took three into Gethsemane, not twelve?
  3. What would it take for this group to become a place where Level 3 is safe?
