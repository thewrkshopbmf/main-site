Step 1

Find it

The Pattern Finder takes 3–5 minutes and points you to the pattern that shows up most for you.

Step 2

Understand it

Each pattern page shows what may be underneath, where it shows up, and a 7-day practice.

Step 3

Rehearse it

The Interruption Lab walks you through a tense moment so the better response is ready when you need it.

Step 4

Track it

The Growth Journal helps you record real moments, notice what repeats, and review your week. Entries stay on your device.

This is an educational reflection resource, not a diagnostic instrument or a substitute for mental health care. If you face abuse, coercion or unsafe conflict, put your safety first and reach out for appropriate support rather than practicing exposure to harm.
