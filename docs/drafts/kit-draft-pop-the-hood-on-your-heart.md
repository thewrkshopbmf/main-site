Happy 4-3-2-1 Weekend Edition — Training Your Mind One Weekend at a Time

Bro, when was the last time you popped the hood on your heart?

Ever ignore the check engine light in your car?
It doesn’t end well.

Same with your heart and mind. Proverbs 4:23 says:

> “Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.”

That’s not spiritual poetry, that’s an engine manual for your soul.

Because if your spiritual engine is misfiring, everything sputters: relationships, decisions, peace, joy, purpose.

This weekend, let’s give our hearts a tune-up so we’re not stuck on the side of life’s highway.

## 4 Verses to Pop the Hood On
- Proverbs 4:23 – Guard your heart, because EVERYTHING flows from it.
- Psalm 139:23-24 – Invite God to run a diagnostic scan.
- Philippians 4:8 – Filter what fuel you’re putting in.
- Jeremiah 2:13 – Stop digging broken cisterns and drink living water instead.

## 3 Questions for Your Heart Scan
- Where’s my “check engine light” blinking right now?
- What spiritual carbon buildup have I been ignoring?
- Am I running my life on God’s power, or my own fumes?

## 2 Truths to Keep Your Engine Running
- Small leaks become big breakdowns. Don’t ignore the little stuff.
- God’s tune-up always leads to more power, peace, and purpose.

## 1 One-Minute Win
> Pray this right now:
> “Lord, pop the hood on my heart. Search me, clean me, and tune me up for Your purpose. I don’t want to drive another mile with my spiritual engine light on. In Jesus’ name, Amen.”

## Drop the Mic Moment
> “If you wouldn’t drive your truck with the check engine light on… why are you trying to drive your life that way?”
