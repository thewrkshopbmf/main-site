The Prover · Performance and worth

Why do I never feel good enough?

Achievement can become an exhausting substitute for a stable sense of worth.

Start the 7-day practice Not sure this is you? Take the Pattern Finder
What it looks like

Connecting personal worth to achievement

What may feel threatened

  • Being overlooked
  • Not measuring up
  • Being ordinary
  • Failing publicly

Common first reactions

  • Work harder immediately
  • Compare myself with others
  • Seek reassurance or recognition
  • Hide mistakes

What often follows

  • Feel briefly validated
  • Stay dissatisfied
  • Become exhausted
  • Set a higher standard
Where it shows up

Where this might show up

At workYou take on every project to prove you deserve promotion.
In relationshipsYou turn family time into another performance metric.
At homeYour child struggles and you treat it as a reflection of your success.

Examples are illustrative, not proof of anyone’s motives. Responding firmly to real mistreatment can be appropriate.

Look beneath the reaction

What would it mean about me if I did not excel today?

Pause and ask: If no one applauded this, would it still be worth doing?

Practice a different response

Measure faithfulness and learning, not only applause.

Choose one meaningful action without measuring your value by the response.
