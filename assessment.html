How to take this honestly

This is not a personality test. It's a spiritual inventory. The goal is not to find out who you want to be — it's to surface what God has already placed in you.

Answer based on your actual experience, not what you wish were true. Think of your life in community, in service, in moments when something felt natural and fruitful — not your best Sunday.

Rate each statement on a scale of 1 to 5:

1Not like me at all — I rarely or never experience this
2Occasionally true, but not consistent
3Sometimes true — I notice it but it's not defining
4Often true — this shows up regularly in my life
5Very much like me — this is consistent and others notice it too

At the end, you'll receive your top gifts, their paired shadow gifts, your gift cluster, and a concrete deployment plan — not just a profile to read and forget.
