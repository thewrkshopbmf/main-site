The Doing vs. the Dwelling: busy for God, or with Him?

A ready-to-run eight-week men’s study that moves from abiding in Jesus to being sent by Him. Hands-on activities in the second half. Free for any men’s group or church.

ShareTextEmail

The teaching

Many ministries teach men how to do more for God. Scripture certainly calls us to serve. But the deeper invitation is this: become the kind of man who walks with God. When a man truly walks with God, service becomes the overflow rather than the substitute.

Abiding isn’t passive. It’s active attentiveness: returning the heart’s attention to Jesus, again and again, all day long. And the greatest threat to intimacy with Jesus isn’t always sin. Sometimes it’s becoming so busy doing things for Him that you stop being with Him.

The eight sessions

  • 1The Activity vs. the Abiding · John 15:1–8
    Two operating systems: “I’m doing things for God,” or “I’m becoming with God.”
  • 2The Service vs. the Surrender · Luke 10:38–42 · Romans 12:1–2
    Serving is easier than sitting at His feet, and the applause makes it addictive.
  • 3The Ministry vs. the Master · Revelation 2:1–5 · Matthew 7:21–23
    A good thing becomes a dangerous thing when it quietly replaces the best thing.
  • 4The Fruit vs. the Effort · John 15:5, 8, 16 · Galatians 5:22–23
    Fruit isn’t forced performance. It’s Christ’s character becoming visible, and it contains seed.
  • 5The Performance vs. the Presence · Exodus 33:7–18 · Psalm 27:4–8
    Performance asks, “How am I doing?” Presence asks, “Who am I with?”
  • 6The Assignment vs. the Attachment · John 21:15–19 · John 20:19–22 · Matthew 28:18–20
    Jesus asked “Do you love me?” three times before He said “Feed my sheep.” Attachment comes before assignment.
  • 7The Work vs. the Walk · Micah 6:6–8 · Colossians 2:6–7 · Genesis 5:24
    God isn’t asking for more offerings. He’s asking for a walk: His pace, His direction, His company.
  • 8The Sending vs. the Staying · John 20:21 · Matthew 28:19–20 · Acts 1:8
    God forms men in order to send them. A reservoir holds what it receives. A river passes it on.

Sessions 1–4 dwell on John 15. Sessions 5–8 are hands-on: silence, prayer walks, object lessons, a commissioning circle and a quick check men take on their phones.

What’s in the kit

  • Leader guide: all eight sessions, with what to say, what to ask, Scripture, room activities and a weekly practice.
  • Participant handout: notes for each session, tap-through quick checks and activity sheets, the Return practice and a weekly Return log.
