Step 4 · Remain · Seven days

Seven days of prayer.

One passage. One thought. One question. One practice. Start anywhere. Repeat a day. Miss a day. You're not behind.

Day 1 Start with “Our Father”
“Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name…”Matthew 6:9

Think: The prayer Jesus taught never says “I,” “me” or “my.” You can pray it alone. You're never praying it as a man alone.

Ask: Am I praying to a Father, or performing for a judge?

Do: Pray the Lord's Prayer slowly (Matthew 6:9–13). Stop on “Our.” Name one other man and pray it for him too.

Go deeper: What Is Biblical Prayer? →

Day 2 Say what you actually want
Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.Philippians 4:6

Think: In Gethsemane, Jesus said plainly, “Take this cup from me.” Surrender doesn't start with pretending you don't want anything.

Ask: What do I want that I've never actually said to God?

Do: Write the real request in one sentence. No church words. Then thank Him for one thing, even if nothing has changed.

Go deeper: How Do I Know What to Pray For? →

Day 3 Look underneath it
You do not have because you do not ask God.James 4:2

Think: Before James warns about wrong motives, he names a simpler problem: not asking at all. Some of us are fighting for what we never once asked God for.

Ask: What would getting this prove about me?

Do: Under yesterday's sentence, write every reason you want it. Even the ugly ones. Bring the whole list to God.

Find your pattern →

Day 4 Give it to God, and stay awake
Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you.1 Peter 5:7

Think: Casting your worry is how you humble yourself (verse 6). And the next line says, “Be alert.” Surrender isn't checking out.

Ask: What do I keep picking back up?

Do: Sort it into four columns: mine to do, mine to influence, theirs to decide, God's to determine.

Use the Surrender Tool →

Day 5 Pray the hard question
How long, LORD? Will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me?Psalm 13:1

Think: Lament isn't the opposite of faith. It's faith that refuses to stop talking to God. Jesus prayed a lament from the cross.

Ask: What have I concluded about God from what hasn't happened?

Do: Write four lines that start with “How long…” End with Psalm 13:5, or leave it unfinished like Psalm 88. Both are prayers.

Go deeper: When God Seems Silent →

Day 6 Get up
“Rise! Let us go! Here comes my betrayer!”Mark 14:42

Think: Jesus surrendered, then walked toward the hardest thing in front of Him. Surrender didn't take Him out of the garden. It sent Him into the night.

Ask: What's one thing that is clearly mine to do?

Do: Do it today. If anyone is in danger, getting help is the step.

Go deeper: What Do I Do After I Give It to God? →

Day 7 Remain
“I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing.”John 15:5

Think: Prayer isn't a project you finish. It's how a branch stays connected to the vine.

Ask: Where am I trying to produce fruit on my own?

Do: Tell one brother what you've been praying about this week. Pray the Lord's Prayer together. Then start again at day 1 whenever you're ready.
