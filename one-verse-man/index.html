Suggested group experience

  1. Share the diagnostic link with participants in advance. Participation is optional.
  2. Ask everyone to reflect privately on one pattern that stood out. Do not require disclosure of scores.
  3. Lead the teaching using the facilitator guide.
  4. Invite discussion in groups of three to five with respectful confidentiality expectations.
  5. Close with one practical step, prayer, and an optional seven-day follow-up.
