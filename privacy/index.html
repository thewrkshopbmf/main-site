Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

A plain-language account of the information thewrkshop.com collects, why, and how to get off any list. Last updated October 2026.

The short version

  • Most of our reflection tools keep your answers in your own browser. They are never sent to us.
  • When you give us your email, phone number or a message, we use it only for the thing you asked for.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't share your phone number with anyone for marketing.
  • You can ask to be removed from any list at any time.

Reflection tools: your answers stay with you

The Pattern Finder, Pattern Interruption Lab, Growth Journal, Are You Missing Your Moments?, The Belief Project, What’s Really First?, The Unexamined Heart, After the Amen and the Prayer Formation tools run in your browser. What you select or type is not sent to TheWrkShop. Some tools (such as the Growth Journal) save your entries on your own device so you can come back to them; clearing your browser data removes them.

Some of these tools offer an optional 7-day email path. If you sign up, we keep your email address, your first name if you give it, and the name of the theme the email path follows. We don't receive your answers.

Spiritual Gifts Assessment

The Spiritual Gifts Assessment saves your answers and results without your name so you can pick up where you left off. If you ask us to email your results, we send them to the address you give, and a copy goes to TheWrkShop so we can help you find a place to serve.

When you give us your details

  • The Daily by email. Your email address and, if you give it, your first name. We send a confirmation email first, then The Daily each weekday. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • The Daily by text. If you receive The Daily by text message, we keep your name and phone number to send it. Message and data rates may apply. To stop, reply to any text or contact us and we'll remove you.
  • Newsletter. Your email address, to send occasional updates about new resources, groups and guides. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
  • Get Known (group intake). Your name, contact details and the answers you choose to give. Only TheWrkShop's leaders see them, and we use them to set up a conversation and find the right group.
  • Contact form. Your name, email and message, so we can reply.

What the website itself collects

  • Visit counts. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages and tools are helpful. It may set cookies in your browser and records things like pages visited, approximate location and device type. It also records actions such as “signed up for The Daily” or “finished the Pattern Finder,” but never your answers.
  • Fonts. Some site fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested).

Who handles your information

We use a few trusted services to run the site: Netlify (hosting), Supabase (our database), Resend (sending email), Kit (the newsletter) and Google Analytics (visit counts). They process information only to provide those services to us.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom, stop texts by replying to one, or contact us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Our resources are written for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

This isn't emergency help

TheWrkShop's resources are for spiritual reflection. They aren't counseling or therapy, and nobody monitors this site for emergencies. If you're in danger, call 911. If you're thinking about suicide, or worried about someone who is, call or text 988 in the U.S. any time.

Questions about this page? Contact us. If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date above.
