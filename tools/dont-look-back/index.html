Remember Lot’s Wife

Don’t look back. Salt or shine?

“Remember Lot’s wife!”

Luke 17:32

She left Sodom with her feet, but not with her heart. A private, interactive reflection on what you’ve been preserving, and what it’s costing you today.

Share Text Email
The teaching

Salt preserves

Yes, Lot’s wife disobeyed God’s word, and that matters. But look closer. She became salt, and salt was used to make things last past their natural life. When we can’t let go of the past, we bring it into the present, which means it comes with us into the future. We’re preserving it.

We preserve hurt and ill will. We preserve trauma and triggers. We even preserve good memories that start competing with what God wants to do next. Israel left Egypt, but their hearts stayed there. In the wilderness they missed the fish and forgot the chains.

Remember Lot’s wife. When Jesus taught about the day He returns, He could have said, “Remember Noah” or “Remember Lot.” He said, “Remember Lot’s wife” (Luke 17:32). She escaped the city, but she never escaped the attachment. Proximity to deliverance isn’t the same as surrender.

Root check. Surface: “I’m just processing.” Deeper: “I keep it alive because, in a strange way, the replay feels good.” The truth that breaks it: every morning God hands you new mercy and a new 24 hours. Yesterday doesn’t get to spend them unless you let it.

For leaders

Use this with a men’s group

Invite men to take the reflection before you meet. Don’t ask anyone to share results. Teach from Genesis 19:15–26 and Luke 17:26–33, then let men share what they choose. Be gentle with men carrying real trauma: letting go never means pretending it didn’t happen.

A 60-minute gathering

  • 5 minWelcome. Read aloud: “When you preserve your pain, you also preserve your potential.”
  • 10 minObject lesson: pour salt over a slice of fresh fruit and set a seed next to it. Salt keeps things from spoiling, and from growing. Then teach Genesis 19 and Luke 17:32.
  • 10 minThe 10-Credit Audit together: each man lays out 10 coins and hands over what the past takes each morning
  • 20 minGroups of three: “What have I left on the outside but still hold on the inside?”
  • 10 minMirror Moment: each man writes See it, Say it, Shift it, Seal it on a card
  • 5 minPray Philippians 3:13–14 over each other

Discussion prompts

  1. Lot’s wife left Sodom but still looked back. What have you left that you still look back at?
  2. Which is harder for you to let go of: a bad memory or a good season? Why?
  3. What would you do with three or four more credits a day?
